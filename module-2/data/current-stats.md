# Riverside Public Library: how it runs today

*A fictional library. These numbers come from the shared spreadsheet and the front-desk tally,
last 12 months.*

## Size
- 1,180 active members. 22% are children. 14% have no email address on file.
- 14,600 book copies across about 11,200 titles (so most titles have one copy, a few have many).
- Open Monday to Saturday. Closed Sundays and about 10 public holidays a year.

## Traffic
- About 190 loans per weekday, about 320 on Saturdays.
- Peaks: weekdays 12:00-14:00, Saturdays 10:00-13:00.
- Two front-desk computers, plus one staff member working in the back.

## Returns and fines
- 17% of loans are returned late. Median lateness is 6 days.
- 2% of loans are still not returned after 60 days.
- About EUR 3,100 in fines collected per year.
- Staff waive roughly 40% of fines at their own discretion ("goodwill").

## Pain points
- Everything lives in one shared spreadsheet. Two staff overwrote each other's edits
  3 times last year.
- Overdue reminders are done by hand, by phone or postcard: about 15 staff hours per week.
- Nobody can tell a member quickly whether a title is available.

## What's coming
- A second branch opens in about 12 months. It would share the catalog.
